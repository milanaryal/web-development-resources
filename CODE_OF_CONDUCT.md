# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the repository. Harassment,
personal attacks, discrimination, spam, deceptive promotion, and repeated
disregard of maintainer guidance are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may restrict participation when
needed to protect contributors and the project. Report non-sensitive conduct
concerns through a GitHub issue. Use GitHub's abuse-reporting tools for threats,
harassment, or other platform-level concerns; do not publish private or sensitive
information in an issue.
